# Vulnerability Management Workflow

## Step 1: Asset Inventory
Identify applications, devices and software that need protection.

## Step 2: Authorised Scanning
Perform security checks only on systems with written permission.

## Step 3: Severity Assessment
Classify findings as Critical, High, Medium or Low.

## Step 4: Exposure and Business Impact
Understand the affected component and possible business impact.

## Step 5: Remediation Ownership
Assign each finding to a responsible team member.

## Step 6: Retesting
Repeat the relevant security test after applying a fix.

## Step 7: Exception Handling
Document accepted risks, reasons, approvals and review dates.

## Step 8: Documentation
Maintain test evidence, remediation records and final reports.
